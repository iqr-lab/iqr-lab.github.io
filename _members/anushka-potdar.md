Anushka Potdar is a fourth year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Yale University, advised by Tesca Fitzgerald. Her research aims to create AI diagnostic tools to help astronauts solve unexpected problems in space. Outside of her Ph.D. research, Anushka serves as Chair of Yale's Graduate Student Assembly, as a Fellow of Yale’s Graduate Writing Lab, and as a Graduate Affiliate of Yale’s Trumbull College.
